The C*lour of Pomegranates

How can the asterisk be used as a symbol of censorship? This book is a study of Sergei Parajanov’s The Color of Pomegranates, a Soviet-Armenian film that depicts the life of 18th-century poet and musician Sayat Nova. Due to heavy suppression by the Soviet Union, many aspects of the film were changed or scrubbed, and the asterisks serve as a visual depiction of that erasure.

( Specs )Duplex printing on inkjet
8 × 5”
Mohawk (Via Vellum) Bright White

The Gestalt Series

A collectible trading card series that visually depicts five selected Gestalt Principles: Proximity, Continuity, Similarity, Closure, and Figure-Ground. Every colorway is divided in half to segue to the next, establishing the dependency each principle has on the others for its holistic effectiveness.

Information( About )

Jeannie Han is a graphic designer based in New York/New Jersey.

She imagines design to be one of many ways to implement simplistic thinking into creating systems for complex/abstract ideas. The intersection of art and systems produces what is, to her, deemed functional and successful work. Her keen interests in art created through typography supports her ideas conceptually, while her passion for form–making creates multi–dimensional networks of interpretation.
