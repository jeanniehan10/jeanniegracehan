CLUTTER IS EVERYWHERE.

Many perceive clutter as a physical phenomenon, but how could clutter also exist in our mental space?

‘CLUTTER as a T( )L: [An Index for Messy Pedagogy]’ is a five-part dos-à-dos, Coptic-bound book that physically and conceptually embodies clutter.

How To Declutter:

Over the course of 12 months, research surrounding art, nature, history, and science was gathered and embedded throughout the pages.

Each research topic, whether it be excerpts from sourced articles, images and their descriptions, or fragments of larger images, was separated into pieces of information. Each piece was assigned a ‘key’ and ‘object number’ that led to the next step in further understanding what you were consuming.

Hundreds of these journeys unfold in varying frequencies and orders, even occasionally intersecting with one another to form a complex visual information system that can only be deciphered through the act of ‘decluttering’.

What’s The Point?

Many of us struggle with the dopamine chase and instant gratification. This book is meant to represent the exact opposite of those two things. It requires immense patience, slow observation, and effort to decipher. However, since it is a book requiring labor to read, what you learn from its contents is much more rewarding than any social media doomscroll.
