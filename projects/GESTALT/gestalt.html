The Gestalt Principles of art and design is a set of laws of human perception that helps us understand visually complex imagery and compositions in everyday life. Understanding these principles is incredibly essential in establishing hierarchy, clarity, and aesthetically pleasing and intuitive design.

The Gestalt Series is a collectible trading card series that visually depicts 5 selected Gestalt Principles (Proximity, Continuity, Similarity, Closure, and Figure-Ground). Every colorway is divided in half to segue to the next colorway, establishing the dependency each principle has on each other for its holistic effectiveness. Whether these cards are simply for collecting or serve as an important tool for learning, The Gestalt Series is an ever-growing collectible series for creatives and the general consumer interested in art and aesthetics.
