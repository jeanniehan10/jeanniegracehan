Digital Printing

Dimensions: 8.5 x 11”

Libretto design for Anne Martine Whitehead’s FORCE! an opera in three acts.

Published under The Vera List Center for Art and Politics at The New School.

A written script with accompanying footnotes, “FORCE! is a performance of strange sisterhoods with the power to disintegrate walls blooming in the shadows of the prison industrial complex.”

This design features typographic layout and cover design refinements made post-publication.
