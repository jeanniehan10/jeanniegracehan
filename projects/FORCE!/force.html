Libretto for Anna Martine Whitehead’s FORCE! an opera in three acts. Published under The Vera List Center for Art and Politics at The New School.

A written script with accompanying footnotes, FORCE! an opera in three acts features a performance of strange sisterhood with the power to navigate through and against the shadows existing within the prison industrial complex.
